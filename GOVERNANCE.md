# Governance

Ayni is an early-stage, maintainer-led open-source project. Its technical
direction is vendor-neutral: no employer, customer, or vendor has special
project control.

## Roles

- **Contributors** participate through issues, discussions, documentation, and
  pull requests.
- **Reviewers** are contributors trusted by maintainers to review a defined
  area.
- **Maintainers** steward the project, approve releases and compatibility
  changes, and are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Decisions

Routine changes are decided in public pull requests. Changes to security,
compatibility, releases, project goals, governance, or the roadmap require an
explicit maintainer decision recorded in the relevant issue or pull request.
Maintainers seek consensus; where consensus is not reached, a maintainer makes
and records the decision. A maintainer must recuse from decisions where a
personal or employer interest conflicts with the project's interests.

## Maintainer lifecycle

A maintainer may nominate a contributor based on sustained, constructive work
and demonstrated stewardship. Existing maintainers decide appointments,
responsibility changes, inactive status, removal, and emeritus recognition in a
recorded repository decision. Maintainers update their listed affiliation or
contact details within 30 days of a relevant change.

## Changes to this governance

Governance changes require a public pull request, at least seven days for
comment, and approval by all active maintainers. Material changes are announced
through the project's public communication channels.
